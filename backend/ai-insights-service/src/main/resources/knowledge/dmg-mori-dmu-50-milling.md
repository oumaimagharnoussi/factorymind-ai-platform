The DMU 50 is a five-axis universal milling machine with a 14,000 rpm motor spindle. Sensors: spindle temperature (warning 75 °C, critical 90 °C) and spindle vibration (warning 4.5 mm/s, critical 7.1 mm/s).

## Safety

Switch the machine to setup mode or switch it off and apply lockout/tagout before reaching into the work area. Chips are sharp and coolant can irritate skin: wear gloves and glasses. Never run the spindle with the doors open.

## Spindle

### Spindle vibration rising

1. Check the tool and tool holder first: unbalanced or damaged tools, dirty taper, wrong overhang. Run the spindle without a tool at 6,000 rpm: if vibration disappears, the problem is the tool.
2. Clean the spindle taper and check the drawbar clamping force (needs the service kit).
3. If vibration remains without a tool and rises with temperature, the spindle bearings are worn. Spindle bearings are not replaced on site: the spindle goes back to the manufacturer for overhaul. Plan it with the engineer.

### Spindle temperature high

Check the spindle chiller: water level, filter, fan, and that it is switched on. Run the warm-up program after any stop longer than 2 hours.

## Coolant

Keep the emulsion concentration between 6 and 8 % (refractometer reading). Low concentration causes corrosion and short tool life; bacterial growth shows as a bad smell and needs a full tank cleaning.

## Preventive maintenance

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Coolant level and concentration, clean chips, check way lubrication oil level |
| Weekly | Clean chip conveyor, check spindle chiller filter |
| 6 months | Check axes backlash and the geometry with a ballbar test |
