The CR 10 is a vertical multistage centrifugal pump that supplies coolant to machining line C. Sensors: motor temperature (warning 75 °C, critical 90 °C) and vibration (warning 4.5 mm/s, critical 7.1 mm/s).

## Safety

Isolate the motor electrically with lockout/tagout, close the suction and discharge valves and drain the pump through the drain plug before opening it. Coolant may be hot.

## Troubleshooting

### Vibration and noise like gravel

This is cavitation: the pressure at the pump inlet is too low. Check that the suction valve is fully open, the suction strainer is clean, and the coolant tank level is above the minimum. Running dry destroys the shaft seal within minutes.

### Shaft seal leaking

A few drops per hour are normal for a mechanical seal; a steady leak means the seal is worn. Replace the cartridge shaft seal (kit for CR 10 HQQE); the pump does not need to be removed from the pipework.

### Motor hot

Check the pump is not running against a closed discharge valve and that the duty point is within the pump curve.

## Preventive maintenance

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Weekly | Check for leaks, noise, strainer pressure drop |
| Yearly | Check motor bearings and insulation, replace shaft seal if leaking |
