The GA 37 is an oil-injected rotary screw compressor of 37 kW delivering compressed air at 7.5 bar to the plant network. Sensors: element outlet temperature (warning 75 °C on the FactoryMind sensor, the compressor's own controller stops at 110 °C) and vibration on the motor (warning 4.5 mm/s, critical 7.1 mm/s).

## Safety

Stop the compressor with the stop button and wait for the unloading cycle to finish, then isolate electrically and close the outlet valve. The oil separator vessel stays under pressure for a few minutes: check that the pressure gauge reads 0 bar before opening any plug. Oil and element surfaces can be above 80 °C.

## Troubleshooting

### High element temperature

1. Check the oil level in the sight glass with the compressor stopped and depressurised.
2. Clean the oil cooler and air cooler (compressed air from inside out, or a cooler cleaner).
3. Check the room ventilation: the cooling air inlet must stay below 40 °C; hot air must not recirculate.
4. Replace the oil filter and check the thermostatic bypass valve, which can stay open and bypass the cooler.

### Oil carry-over in the air network

The oil separator element is saturated or its scavenge line is blocked. Replace the separator element and clean the scavenge line restrictor.

## Preventive maintenance

| Interval | Task |
| --- | --- |
| Daily | Drain condensate, check display for warnings |
| 2,000 h | Replace air filter and oil filter |
| 4,000 h | Replace oil (Roto-Inject Fluid) and oil separator element |
| 8,000 h | Check motor bearings and the drive coupling |
